How a job runs

The same five steps whether it is a five-page site or a full day of shooting.

  1. Scope

    A call or a coffee. I ask what the work has to achieve, look at what exists and send a written quote.

  2. Plan

    A page list, a shot list and a short checklist of what I need from you, agreed before anything is built.

  3. Build and shoot

    You see the work on a private link as it comes together, on your own phone, not in a slideshow.

  4. Launch

    Redirects from old addresses, testing on real devices, and the site submitted to Google and Bing.

  5. Look after it

    Updates, fixes and changes handled as they come up, on a plan or by the hour.

Questions

What does Max K Media do?

Three things that usually take three suppliers: websites and online stores, commercial photography and video, and industrial automation. It is one person, Max Krause, working from Bathurst.

Which areas do you cover?

Bathurst is home. I work with businesses in Orange, Lithgow, Mudgee, Dubbo, Oberon, Blayney and Cowra, and the villages around them. Web work can be done for clients anywhere in Australia. See the service areas.

How much does a website cost?

Work is quoted per project after a short scoping call, based on $180 an hour plus GST. The quote lists what is included, so you can trim or add before anything starts. The guide to website costs in regional NSW explains what moves the price up or down.

Can you take the photos for my website as well?

Yes, and it is the reason the two services sit together. The shoot is planned around the pages that need images, so nothing is cropped awkwardly to fit a layout it was never taken for.

Do you look after websites you did not build?

Yes. I start with a review of what is there, then quote for the fixes or take over upkeep. Details are on the support page.

Have a job in mind?

Send the details and I will come back with questions or a quote. No account managers, no hand-offs.
