up front.

The things that usually surface halfway through a contract.

01

Response, not resolution

The clock covers how fast I'm on it. How long a fix takes depends on what broke — I won't promise a repair time for a fault I haven't seen.

02

Included hours

Callout hours cover fixes and small changes. A redesign or a new feature is quoted separately, so you're never billed for a project out of a support budget.

03

You still own everything

Repo, domain, hosting account, all in your name. Cancel and nothing is held hostage — that's the point of building it that way.

04

Leaving

Thirty days notice, no exit fee. If you're going elsewhere I'll hand over documentation to whoever's next.
