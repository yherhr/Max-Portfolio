The Max K Media website has been rebuilt from scratch. The old one grew a section at a time: web first, then photography, then automation. This one was planned as a single site, so it is easier to see what I do and where I work.

## What is new

- **Case studies.** [Four jobs written up](/work.html) with what was built and how: CME Detailing, Rural Asset Protection Solutions, BX Realty and Rokiovertime.
- **Guides.** [Six plain-English guides](/guides.html) answering the questions that come up before most builds and shoots, starting with [what a website costs in regional NSW](/guides/website-cost-regional-nsw.html).
- **Photography by subject.** Separate pages for [automotive](/photography/automotive.html), [apparel and product](/photography/apparel-product.html), [sport and fitness](/photography/sport-fitness.html) and [events and portraits](/photography/events-portraits.html).
- **Video.** It has [its own page](/video.html) now.
- **Service areas.** [One page](/locations.html) for the eight Central West towns I travel to.

## What did not change

Every address from the old site still works. Pages that were retired send you to the closest current page, so old links and bookmarks still land somewhere useful. It is the same method I use on client sites, set out in [how to move a website without losing your Google rankings](/guides/move-website-without-losing-rankings.html).

## What this blog is for

The guides cover questions that do not change much from year to year. This is for everything with a date on it: sites going live, recent shoots, and notes on problems worth writing down.

If you find something broken on the new site, [tell me](/contact.html) and I will fix it.
